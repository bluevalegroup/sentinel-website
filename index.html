02 // 04 THE PROBLEM & CORE POSITIONING

Every camera records. Few systems can explain.

Modern security environments generate enormous amounts of video. Security teams operate across increasingly complex environments with cameras generating vast amounts of footage every single day.

The challenge isn't having cameras. It's finding what matters. Finding a specific person, vehicle, event or sequence can require hours of manual review across dozens of disconnected video feeds.

When an incident happens, operators shouldn't have to spend hours manually searching through footage to understand what happened. Sentinel turns video into searchable intelligence.

Operational Paradigm Comparison
Legacy Video Systems [OBSOLETE]
  • ✕ Operators manually fast-forward through hundreds of hours of raw footage.
  • ✕ Keywords limited to rigid camera channel IDs and unreliable timestamps.
  • ✕ Zero cross-camera object re-identification; context lost across sector boundaries.
  • ✕ Critical incidents discovered hours or days after perimeter breaches occur.
Sentinel Video Intelligence [ACTIVE]
  • ✓ Natural language search queries index and isolate footage in sub-second time.
  • ✓ Deep scene understanding extracts clothing, vehicle models, gestures, and access events.
  • ✓ Automated multi-camera trajectory synthesis and cross-facility target tracking.
  • ✓ Immediate anomaly detection continuously surfacing uncharacteristic behavior in real time.
